# Croatia Adventure — external playtest guide

Thank you for testing! The game is a five-day comedy holiday in Dalmatia. It takes about **10 minutes per holiday**, and every holiday is different.

## How to play on an Android phone

1. Open the playtest link in **Chrome** and tap the **Croatia Adventure** card (or add `/#adventure` to the link).
2. Optional — install it like an app: Chrome menu **⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app**. It then opens full-screen and keeps your progress.
3. Choose **Today's adventure** (the same for everyone today) or **Free play** (a new random holiday every time).

Your progress (tips, hidden discoveries, achievements) is saved on your phone only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there are no accounts.

## Please play at least three holidays

Try different styles, for example:
1. **Play naturally**, choosing whatever you would really do.
2. **Play cheap**: hostel, bus, free beaches.
3. **Play chaotic**: always pick the funniest-sounding option.

## After each holiday

At the bottom of the ending screen, tap **“Playtesting? Copy your run log for feedback”** and paste it into your message (WhatsApp or email). The log contains only the game's random seed, your choices and your result — no personal data.

Then answer, briefly:

1. **Did you want to start another holiday straight away?** Why, or why not?
2. Which **moment made you laugh** (or smile)?
3. Which moment was **boring, confusing or felt pointless**?
4. Did any choice feel like it **didn't matter**?
5. Did you notice something from an **earlier day coming back later**? Was that fun?
6. Did you find a **🔎 hidden discovery**? Did it feel rewarding?
7. **Duje**, the narrator: funny, annoying, or both? Does he feel like one consistent person?
8. Was your **holiday story title** and the five-day diary at the end something you would share?
9. Were the **Local Insider tip cards** useful? Did any seem wrong?
10. Anything that looked **broken** on your phone (text cut off, buttons hard to tap, slow)?

**Croatian testers:** please also skim [`LOCAL_REVIEW.md`](LOCAL_REVIEW.md), and tell us about any phrase, custom or joke that sounds wrong, unnatural or unfair to a local.

## Known limitations of this build

- The story and in-game prices are **fiction**. Only the tip cards are real-world information, and each is marked “Verified” or “Editorial”.
- Some editorial tips that state rules or customs are still **awaiting local or official review** (listed in `docs/UNVERIFIED.md`).
- There are no accounts, leaderboards or multiplayer; Supabase is deliberately not enabled yet.
